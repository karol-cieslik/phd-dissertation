copyrights, trademarks, or institutional insignia.

The following files are **excluded from the MIT grant pending a provenance
and permission review**:

- `uj_logo.jpg` — example university crest/mark. Use and redistribution may
  be restricted by the institution; its presence does not signal that this is
  an endorsed or official university template.
- `figures/introduction/annealing-intro.png`
- `figures/experimental/electron-beam-heating.png`
- `figures/results/some-graph.png`

For these excluded files, the repository currently makes **no representation
that reuse or redistribution is permitted**. They remain in the example
project to demonstrate image placement, but users preparing their own theses
should replace them with images and marks they are authorized to use.

A future rights audit may determine that some figures can be licensed under
MIT or a suitable Creative Commons license. Until then, do not treat an
unverified image's inclusion here as a permission grant.

The template is unofficial. Users must also confirm their doctoral school's
current formatting and submission requirements. If you identify a
misattributed or inappropriately included asset, please open a GitHub issue
so it can be reviewed.
